# WhyPoor — Support

Need help with WhyPoor? You're in the right place.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How does WhyPoor store my data?**
All your expenses and categories are stored privately in your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. WhyPoor has no server of its own — we never see or have access to your data. It syncs automatically across your devices as long as you're signed into the same iCloud account.

**How do I change my currency?**
Open Settings (gear icon) from the main screen and select your currency from the list.

**How does the billing period work?**
WhyPoor lets you set a custom "salary day" in Settings. Instead of tracking calendar months, your budget periods run from that day to the day before it in the following month — matching when you actually get paid.

**Can I import expenses from another app?**
Yes. Go to Settings → Import Expenses (CSV) and select a CSV file exported from another budgeting app. You'll be able to map the file's columns to the right fields before importing.

**Can I export my data?**
Yes. From the Expenses breakdown screen, tap the export icon to generate a CSV file of your expenses for the selected date range, which you can save, email, or share.

**Can I add expenses with Siri?**
Yes. Try saying "Hey Siri, add an expense in WhyPoor" and follow the prompts, or use the Shortcuts app to build your own automation.

**I found a bug or have a feature request.**
