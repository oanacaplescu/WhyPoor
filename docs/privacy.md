# WhyPoor — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [6th of August, 2026]*

WhyPoor ("the app," "we," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This page explains what information the app handles and how.

## Summary

WhyPoor does not operate any servers of its own, does not sell or share your data, and does not use advertising or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The information you enter into the app — your expenses, categories, and settings — is stored using Apple's CloudKit, inside your own private iCloud account. We, the developers, have no access to it.

## What information the app handles

- **Expense data** (amounts, categories, dates, optional descriptions) that you enter directly into the app.
- **App settings** such as your selected currency and salary/billing day.

This information is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed into iCloud, synced privately through Apple's CloudKit to your other devices signed into the same Apple ID. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Data you choose to export or import

If you use the CSV export feature, a file containing your expense data is generated on your device. You control where that file is saved or shared (e.g. via Files, Mail, AirDrop) — WhyPoor does not upload it anywhere.

If you use the CSV import feature, the file you select is read and processed entirely on your device to create expense entries; the file itself is not transmitted anywhere by the app.

## Siri and Shortcuts

If you use Siri or the Shortcuts app to add expenses, that interaction is handled by Apple's on-device Siri and App Intents frameworks. WhyPoor does not receive or store your voice audio; only the resulting data (e.g. an amount and category) is saved the same way as data entered manually.

## Third parties

WhyPoor does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The only external service used is Apple's iCloud/CloudKit, governed by [Apple's own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children's privacy

WhyPoor is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
